# YODY

> Category: Brands

> Surface: web

*Look Good - Feel Good*

YODY — thương hiệu thời trang Việt Nam uy tín, mang áo thun, polo, jeans và trang phục hằng ngày chất liệu tốt, dịch vụ tốt đến tận tay khách hàng. Hotline: 1800 2086.

## Voice & Tone

- **Adjectives:** friendly, practical, optimistic, approachable, everyday-fashion
- **Tone:** Warm Vietnamese retail voice — clear product names, benefit-led headlines, seasonal outfit tips. Confident but never luxury-exclusive. Bilingual brand line in English; commerce copy in Vietnamese.

### Messaging pillars
- Look Good - Feel Good
- Chất liệu tốt · dịch vụ tốt
- Thời trang hằng ngày cho cả gia đình (Nam · Nữ · Trẻ em)
- Ưu đãi & BST theo mùa
- Gợi ý phối đồ thực tế

### Vocabulary
- **Use:** Look Good - Feel Good, chất liệu, thoáng mát, bền vững, phối đồ, BST, ưu đãi, slim fit, form chuẩn, mặc đẹp mỗi ngày
- **Avoid:** haute couture, exclusive drop jargon, cold corporate tone, overly technical fabric jargon without benefit

## Imagery

- **Style:** Bright e-commerce fashion photography — clean studio product shots plus lifestyle campaign banners. High-key backgrounds, true-to-color garments, model-on-white or outdoor seasonal sets.
- **Subjects:** product flat/on-model apparel (áo thun, sơ mi, jeans, polo), seasonal collection heroes (winter/office/sport), family-friendly menswear, womenswear, kids, outfit-styling editorial cards
- **Treatment:** Soft natural light, minimal retouching, generous crop for product detail. Campaign banners full-bleed; PLP cards 3:4 product frames. Gold sale chips and indigo wordmark over light canvas.
- **Avoid:** dark moody luxury fashion, heavy grain / film filters, cluttered lifestyle with competing logos, low-res marketplace stock

## Layout

- **Radius:** 8px
- **Border weight:** 1px
- **Spacing:** 8px baseline grid

### Posture rules
- Light-first commerce UI: white canvas, gray-50 surfaces, ink navy type — never invert to pure black chrome for default marketing pages.
- Radius scale from site tokens: xs 4px · sm 8px (default controls/cards) · md 12px · lg 16px · full pills 9999px.
- Primary CTA uses YODY Gold (#fab005 / surface #ffc84a); hyperlinks and secondary brand accents use Indigo (#3f3f99); logo wordmark lockup at #2A2A86.
- Sticky top header, 1px bottom border, Manrope across UI; touch targets ~40–44px.
- Product grids: 3:4 image cards, muted meta, gold sale badges; collection rows full-bleed horizontal banners.
- Dense retail navigation (Nam / Nữ / Trẻ em mega-menu) with icon+label category chips on surface gray.
